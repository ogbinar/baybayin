Discover your name in Baybayin

Pantig starts with how you pronounce your name, breaks it into syllables, and shows a suggested Baybayin spelling.

How it works

  1. Enter your name.
  2. Choose the pronunciation closest to yours.
  3. See your Baybayin result.
  4. Learn how each pantig was written.
  5. Copy, save, or share it.

For example, Michel may be pronounced “Misyel” or “Mikel,” so Pantig asks before showing a result.

Pantig is a learning and transliteration aid, not an authority that declares one official spelling.

Sources include the National Museum of the Philippines, the Unicode Standard, and the NCCA Philippine History Source Book.
