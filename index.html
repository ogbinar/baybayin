See your name in Baybayin

Pantig makes a best pronunciation guess, breaks it into syllables, and immediately shows one suggested modern Baybayin spelling.

How it works

  1. Enter your name.
  2. See Pantig's suggested Baybayin result.
  3. Check or change the pronunciation Pantig used.
  4. Learn how each pantig was written.
  5. Copy, save, or share it.

For example, Pantig may first read Michel as “Misyel.” Change the pronunciation to “Mikel” if that is closer to how the name is said.

Pantig is a learning and transliteration aid, not an authority that declares one official spelling.

Sources include the National Museum of the Philippines, the Unicode Standard, and the NCCA Philippine History Source Book.
